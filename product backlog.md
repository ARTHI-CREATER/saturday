# Product Backlog – Student Management Portal

## Project Description
The Student Management Portal is a web application that helps students, faculty, and administrators manage academic information such as profiles, attendance, and marks.

## Product Backlog

| ID | User Story | Priority | Story Points |
|----|------------|----------|--------------|
| PB1 | As a student, I want to log in securely. | High | 8 |
| PB2 | As a student, I want to view my profile. | High | 5 |
| PB3 | As a student, I want to update my profile. | Medium | 5 |
| PB4 | As a faculty member, I want to mark attendance. | High | 8 |
| PB5 | As a faculty member, I want to upload marks. | High | 8 |
| PB6 | As a student, I want to view my attendance. | High | 5 |
| PB7 | As a student, I want to view my marks. | High | 5 |
| PB8 | As an administrator, I want to manage student records. | Medium | 13 |
| PB9 | As an administrator, I want to search for students. | Low | 3 |
| PB10 | As a user, I want to view a dashboard. | Medium | 8 |

## Priority Levels

- High – Essential features
- Medium – Important features
- Low – Optional features
